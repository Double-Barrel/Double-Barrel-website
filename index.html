Our Story

A steakhouse in a town of 250.

It started as a burger joint and became the reason folks drive out from Watertown. The room tells the rest — box-car pine, local mounts on every wall, and a Crown Royal collection you won't find twice.

  1. ~2010
    The Double R opens. Three friends — Eddie, Red, and Ricky — open a burger joint on Main Street in Henry, population about 250.
  2. 2012
    The Back Room. An addition seats 50 more — birthdays, banquets, and card nights ever since.
  3. 2014
    Double Barrel Steakhouse. New name, new focus: hand-cut steaks and prime rib worth a drive.
  4. Today
    Cornie's place. Sole owner-manager, seating for 100, a casino room, Glacial Lakes Distillery on the bar, and a patio out front. Filtered water throughout — the coffee, the ice, and the tea all taste like it. People drive from 60 miles away for the steaks and the salad bar.
The drive is part of the meal. Eighteen miles of Highway 212 from Watertown. People come from Redfield, Brookings, Aberdeen — a 60-mile radius. When you leave, you're already thinking about next time.

Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Is Double Barrel Steakhouse open today?

We're open Tuesday through Sunday. Sunday 11 AM – 8 PM; Tuesday and Wednesday 11 AM – 2 PM and 4 – 8 PM (closed for an afternoon break 2–4); Thursday through Saturday 11 AM – 9 PM. Closed Mondays.

When is prime rib night?

Fridays only — slow-roasted with our house seasoning and homemade au jus, 12 oz for $27 or 16 oz for $39, while it lasts.

What are the nightly specials?

8 oz Sirloin ($24) on Sunday and Tuesday, 14 oz HamBurger Steak ($23) Wednesday and Thursday, prime rib on Fridays, and Steak Specials Saturday — usually more than one cut, posted on our Facebook.

Do you take call-in or carry-out orders?

Yes — call (605) 532-7777 and we'll have it ready. Dine-in and carry-out, with a full bar in house.

Can I book the back room for a party?

Yes. The back room seats about 50 for birthdays, banquets, meetings, and card nights. Call (605) 532-7777 to book a date.

Does every meal come with the salad bar?

Steaks and entrées come with the soup & salad bar, a side, and a dinner roll. Burgers, sandwiches, and baskets don't — you can substitute a side salad for $7, or add the full salad bar as its own item ($16).

Do you have a kids menu?

Yes — for kids 10 and under: chicken tenders, mini corn dogs, mini tacos, fish nuggets, mac & cheese, or grilled cheese, each $5 with small fries and a small pop or milk.

Where is Double Barrel Steakhouse?

106 Main Street in Henry, South Dakota — about 18 miles west of Watertown on Highway 212. Easy parking on Main Street, with room for trucks.
